#### Yes Paragraph

First, write the stand, must be very clear.
- Stand: Yes, I agree with the statement as...
Now we write PEE structure. We need to obtain the point from our contexual knowledge. We follow in Year 3: P2Ex(2)Exp, where there must be 2 clear examples, and either 1 explanation for both of them, or 2 for each them separately
- Point: The aim of the vietcong was to re-unite with South vietnam and **gain independance** under their rule.
- **EX**amples (two): (Specific policies/details, names, dates, Relevant)
	- 20 December 1960: National liberation front (Vietcong) is established in North Vietnam to form a military group to provide men and supplies to support the armed struggle in South vietnam against Diems discrimination agaisnt communist symphatisers
	- The Vietcong carried out **1700 Assassinations on South Vietnam government and civil servants**
- **Ex**planation(How it causes the statement, or intensifies war in this case): The aggression by the Vietcong eventually led to american intervention through gulf of tonkin resolution and escalated the war as American sought to suppress the Vietcong's assault on Diem.

- **Ex**amples : hence the vietcong had to turn their attention to combat against USA's military intervention to support the south vietnam government and thus on 7 Feb 1965, the Vietcong attacks U.S base at pleiku.
- **Ex**planation: This would target the support base behind Diem's government as the Vietcong seeks to topple what they believed was an illegimate government to protect the South Vietnamese from Diem's and USA's corruption in their manipulation of the South Viet.

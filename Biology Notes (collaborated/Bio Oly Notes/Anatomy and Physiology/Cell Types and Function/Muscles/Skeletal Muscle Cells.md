Skeletal muscle, attached to bones, is responsible for skeletal movements. The [peripheral](https://api.seer.cancer.gov/rest/glossary/latest/id/55021d54e4b0c48f31d61808) portion of the [central nervous system](https://api.seer.cancer.gov/rest/glossary/latest/id/542eee9f102c1d14697ef800) ([CNS](https://api.seer.cancer.gov/rest/glossary/latest/id/542eee9f102c1d14697ef800)) controls the skeletal muscles. Thus, these muscles are under conscious, or voluntary, control. The basic unit is the muscle fiber with many nuclei. These muscle fibers are striated (having transverse streaks) and each acts independently of neighboring muscle fibers.


Angiotensin has two forms:
- Angiotensin 1
- Angiotensin 2
Angiotensin starts off as [[Angiotensinogen]], synthesised in the liver


Angiotensin 1 is formed when Renin cleaves the long polypeptide of [[Angiotensinogen]] to form the 10 a.a long angiotensin 1. This enzyme serves purely as a precursor

Angiotensin 2 is activated by action of [[Angiotensin Converting Enzyme]], and serves as a clinical drug under the name Giapreza. It raises blood pressure and decreases blood flow to the kidneys.

It also causes the release of the enzyme [[Aldosterone]], and becomes angiotensin III after its breakdown.

[[Biology MOC]]

Itracullular parasites,
<mark class="hltr-red">Unable to reproduce independently.
Can only replicate inside living host cells.</mark>

Contains nucleic acid enclosed within a protein coat.
Two main types of viruses: **Non-enveloped** vs **Enveloped** viruses.

> Nucleic acid
> Either your [[RNA]] or [[DNA]]
> Why RNA?
> - Mutates more often less stable
> - Allows viruses to adapt and evolve more quickly
> Why DNA?
> - DNA is more stable and mutate less often

They can be either non-enveloped virus, or enveloped virus.

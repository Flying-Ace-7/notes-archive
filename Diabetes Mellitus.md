> [!note] What is diabetes Mellitus?
> It is a disorder in which the body is unable to control its blood glucose concentration wothin its normal range.

There are two types of diabetes, commonly known as type 1 and type 2
### Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus
-> **Caussed by a lcak of insulin production int he body**
Commonly develops early in life, and it is an autoimmune disease, where immune cells of the body attack the beta cells of the pancreas, leading to a decrease in insulin production and secretion.

### Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus
It is caused by **insulin resistance** , occures later in a person;s life
The main problem:  A disorder where the target cells do not respond well to insulin
Later in disease : Beta cells of pancreas are overworked, resulting in a decrease of insulin production and secretion over time.

> [!Help] What is insulin ressitance caused by
> - Reduced sensitiy of insulin receptors
> 	- INsulin receptors on target cells (muscle cells) do not bind well to insulin molecules
> 	- The signal resulting from the binding of insulin is weaker
> - Defects in insulin signalling in the cell
> 	- The signal from the insulin is not transmitted well
> 	- Hence glucose transporters are not inserted inot the cell membrane for glucose uptake (no increase in _facillitated diffusion of glucose_ )


Risk factors of type 2 diabetes include:
- heredity
- Unhealthy diet
- Excess body weigt
- Sedentary lifestyle

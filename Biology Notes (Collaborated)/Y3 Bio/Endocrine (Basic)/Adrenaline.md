Adrenaline is a hormone that is used in short-term stress regulation. it is produced in the adrenal glands


### Effects of adrenaline
- Causes dilation of pupils
- Increases breathing rate/ depth of ventilation
- Hair erectile muscles contracts
- increases rate of blood coagulation
- Increases blood sugar level in the blood,
- increases metabolic rate
- Increases heart rate, blood pressure
- Changes in blood flow patterns

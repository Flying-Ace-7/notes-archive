In the [[Nervous System]] Signal conduction takes place between the neurons of the [Peripheral Nervous system]([[Nervous System#Peripheral Nervous System]]) and the Central nervous System.

## Parts of a neuron
![[Neuron Cell Diagram.png]]

1. Dendrite
	- The dendrite is the starting point of the neuron.
	- It detects signals from either a neuron, or a sensory receptor, and conducts weak electrical signals to the axon hillock for integration
2. Soma/ _Cell Body_ contains cell organelles to support cell function
3. Axon
	1. Conduct Strong electrical signals from the axon hillock to the synaptic terminal (axon terminal).
4. Synaptic Terminal
	1. Synaptic terminal is at the synapse, thr gap between two neurons, for signal conduction into the next cell

Neurons are the basic functional units of the nervous system.
Main functions:
- Signal detection (At dendrites)
- Signal conduction (Within the cell)
- Signal Integration (At axon hillock)
- Singal Transmission (To next cell or effector)
Note that they are unidirectional, and singals can only travel in a single direction.

Axon hillock functions as t he trigger zone.
When the incoming weak electrical singals exceed a certain treshold, a strong electrical signal is generated to be conducted to the axon.
Serves as the decision point where the neuron determines wheter to generate a strong electrical signal.

> [!important] How does signal generation occur
> The membrane at the trigger zone has a lot of sodium ion channels. When the treshold is reached, the sodium ion channels open, causing a high number of sodium ions to enter and a strong electrical signal is generated.
> Which is conducted along the axon.





### Mylein sheath
It is a layer of fatty substance (Lipids) that wrap around layers of the axon.
It insulates the electrical signal during signal conduction in the axon.
In the peripheral nervous system, mylein sheath is formed by schwan cells.

The gaps in the mylein sheath in are known as Nodes of Ravenier. Sodium ion channels are concentrated in those nodes, and hence signal regneration occurs there. Signal conduction is faster in myleinated axons rather than non-myleinated axons.

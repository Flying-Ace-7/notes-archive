What is an endocrine gland?

Endocrine gland is a ductless gland that releases chemical messengers, hormones direclty into the bloodstream. 
In contrast, an exocrine gland is a gland that secretes a useful substance through a duct outside of the body.

> Hormone
> Hormone is a chemical messenger that is very potent and produced in minute qualities to prevent over-stimulation or over-supression. It is released by glands and binds to target cells with the corresponding recpetor, to send a signal in the cell. hormones are necessary to influence growth in an organsim , or to  to maintain homeostasis.

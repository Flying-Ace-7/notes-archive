[[Biology MOC]]


A hormone is a chemical signal molecule (most likely water-soluble protein or a fat-soluble steriod) , which is produced in **minute quantities** by an endocrine gland .
It is<mark class="hltr-yellow"> transported in the bloodstream to its target organ</mark> (long-distance signalling).

### Endocrine signalling
- Protein and Amines
	- Hydrophilic
	- Transported in blood
	- **cannot go past cell membrane**
	- They hence bind to receptors on the cell membrane to exert a respons
- Steriods
	- fat-soluble
	- Hydrophobic
	- Transoprted **by carrier proteins** in blood 
	- they _can_ go past the cell membrane
	- bind to recpetors  in the cell to triger a response

### Significance of hormones

Hormones can influecne the growth and development and activity of an organism.
They act as chemical messeners that help to control and regulate body activities, such as maintaing homeostasis.

Secretion of hormones must be carefully controlled, to prevent the over extertion of the target organs, and to prevent over-stimulation or over- suprression . Inadequate or excess production of hormones may have adverse effects on the body.

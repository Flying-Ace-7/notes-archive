To get a **more stable material to decompose, you would require more energy,** and hence, you require more energy to extract sodium than copper.
Examining the reactoin of metal oxides with carbon, and their stability

We see that for Oxides with cations from $K^+$ to $Al^{3+}$ are not reduced when heating with carbon, as carbon's reactivity is right below aluminum and is not reactive enough to displace them.
> <mark class="hltr-blue">Metal oxides not reduced when heating with carbon, from K -> Al</mark>

The other oxides containing metal cations lower in the series will be reduced, as carbon can displace the metal cation to form carbon dioxide and pure metal.
> Metal oxides reduced with ease when heated with carbon, forming metal and carbon dioxide ($Zn$ ->$Au$)

[[Redox reactions]]


This is a organic reaction, and we shall examine two situations, between a carboxylic acid and alcohol, and between a carboxylic acid and primary/secondary amine



### Acid + alcohol


```smiles
CCC(O)=O.OCC>>CCC(=O)OCC.O
```

This is the main reaction between a propanoic acid and ethanol, forming ethane propanoate.
>[!note] In essence,
>the OH from the carboxylic acid and H from the alcohol is lost to form water, and the O from the alcohol bonds with the 1' carbon on the carboxylic acid, to form an [[Ester]] Linkage

Reaction Condition: Excess $H_2SO_4$ + Heat


### Acid and Amine
 

```smiles
CCC(O)=O.NCC>>CCC(=O)NCC.O
```
THis is the basis for an Acid and amine reaction, the reaction condition is that it needs  heat (reflux) 
The condensation reaction is also reversible.

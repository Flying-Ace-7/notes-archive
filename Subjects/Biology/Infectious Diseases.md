Immune diseases are diseases that are transmitted from one person to another, which triggers immune responses in the body.
 > [!tip] Non-Infectious Disease
 > A non-infectious disease is a disease not caused by a pathogen, cannot be transmitted from one individual to another.
 > Caused by genetic environmental, lifestyle, nutritional or physcological factors.
 > like diabetes insipidus, type 1 and type 2 diabetes and Cancer.
 
 An infectious disease is caused by a pathogen, like a [[Virus]] or a [[Bacteria]] .
 A pathogen can be transmitted from one body to another

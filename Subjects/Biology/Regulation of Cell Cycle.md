[[Biology MOC]]

There are specific checkpoints in a cell cycle used to regulate the cell cycle.

### G1 checkpoint
Occurs : end of g1 in interphase
-> Ensures cell is ready for dna synthesis
- cell contents duplicated
- building block for dna replication are sufficient
- cell size

### G2 checkpoint
check: Dna duplicated accurately
-> repair any errors in DNA
ensure cell is large enough

### M-Phase checkpoint (after Metaphsae)
- ensure alignment of chromosomes on metaphase plate
- ensure all kinetochore microtubules are attatched to kinetochores of sister chromatids

[[Biology MOC]]

Manipulation of an organism's genetic material, and transfering one's genes to another organism.

> [!note] If an organism acquires genes from another person, it is a transgenic organsim


Blurting:

1. We start with a bacterial plasmid and a section of human DNA containing the Insulin Gene
2. We use restriction enzymes to cut the bacterial plasmid and human DNA, isolating the gene of interest, and forming cut complementary restriction sites on the plasmid and the gene. (<mark class="hltr-cyan">Cleaving</mark>)
3. The gene and the plasmid form temporary hydrogend bonds through complementary base pairing at the restriction sites, where the nitrogenous bases are complementary.
4. DNA Ligase is used to later form a permant bonds between the gene and the plasmid, catalysing the formation of the phospho-diester bonds between the nucleotides.
5. This forms the recombinant plasmid, which is reintroduced to the bacteria using heat or electrical shock (increase permeability of the cell membrane)

> [!Note] Why are bacteria good host organisms
> - Easy to cultivate, grow quickly through asexual reproduction
> - contain circular dna that is easier to manipulate



Applications:
- Form transgenic organisms that have resistance to common diseases
- Withstand harsher tempratures
- produce greater yield
- Gene rherapy

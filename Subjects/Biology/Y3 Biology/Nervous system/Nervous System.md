The nervous system consists of two main sub-sytems, Central Nervous System and peripheral nervous system.

- Peripheral nervous system
	- Consists of the nerves that branch out from the brain and spinal cord (CNS)
	
- Central Nervous System (CNS)
	- Consists of the brain and the spinal cord. 
	- Integration centre for electric signal transmission 

### Peripheral Nervous System

Consists of the neves that branch out from the brain and spinal cord.
It allows the CNS to interact with the rest of the body.
It consists of two divisions:
- Sensory/Afferent division
	- Responsible for picking up sensoy stimuli and conduct them to the brain and spinal cord
- Motor/ Efferent division
	- Send information from the Brain/CNS to othe muscle cells/effectors to carry out a motor response.

The Motor division can be further split into two parts, Somatic and Autotmatic.
Somatic nervous system controls voluntary muscle and skeletal movements.
The Automatic nervous system carries out basic body processes like heart beating/breathing/ churning of stomach.
- Symphatetic division of the Automatic nervous system, helps get the body into action during  flight - or - fight situations. (release Adrenaline)
- Parasymphetetic division , helps the body calm down and relax, in rest - and -digest situations.





> [!note] Gilial cells
> Gillial cells are te supporting cells of the nervous system.
> They help to support neuron function. insulsation, and nutrition.
> One example of them are the schwan cells, which form the mylein sheath around neurons and help to insulate electrical signals during signal conduction.



Neuron cells are some of the longest living cells of the body, and are <mark class="hltr-green">amiotic</mark>, meaning they do not undergo cell division. They need a steady supply of oxygen and glucose to support their high energy movements.
The CNS and PNS are made of these neurons, which are responsible for signal transmission.
There are three types of neurons:
- Unipolar Neuron
	- Has a single axon, a single _process_ extending from cell body.
	- Containse dendrites on one end,synaptic terminals on the other end

We can also classify neurons into three types based on their function:
- Sensory neurons
	- Transmit electrical signals from sensory receptors towards CNS
	- _Mostly Unipolar neurons_
- Motor neurons
	- Transmit electrical signals from CNS to effectors
	- Mostly multipolar neurons
- Inter neurons
	- Found in CNS
	- Transmit electrical signals between Sensory and motor neurons.
	- Most abundant Type of neurons

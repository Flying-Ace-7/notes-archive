Glucagon is a hormone secreted by the $\alpha$-cells of the pancreas into the bloodstream. ($\alpha$-cells of the islets of Langerhans in the pancreas)
Functions of Glucagon include:
- Stimulate the conversion of stored glycogen into glucose in the liver (Glycogenolysis)
	- When ther is low blood glucose concentration in blood
		- to maintian homeostasis and ensure that cells get sufficient glucose for cellular respiration to take place
- Increase the synthesis of glucose from amino acids through deanimation, which is converted to urea and de animated amino acids, which s further coonverted to glucose
- Increases the rate of ketogenesis, Breakdown fatty acids into energy at the liver and release alternative fuels (ketones) into the bloodstream.

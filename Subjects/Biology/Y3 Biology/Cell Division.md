### Mitosis
#### Prophase:
Chromatin fibres condense into chromosomes.
Nuclear envelope breaks down, stopping ribosome production
<mark class="hltr-red">Centriole pairs and their associated centrosomes migrate to opposite poles of the cell</mark>
Mitotic spindle begins to form,<mark class="hltr-blue"> centrosomes-> microtubules organising centre</mark>
Microtubules attaches to the kinetochores of chromosomes

##### Metaphase:
Chromosomes are aligned on the metaphase plate
Kinetochore microtubules are attached to the kinetochores on sister chromatids
Non-Kinetochore microtubules overlap at the metaphase plate

#### Anaphase
<mark class="hltr-red"> Centromeres divide alloeing the sister chromatids ot separate</mark>
sister chromatids separate due to the shortening of kinetochore microtubules, homologous (daughter) chromosomes migrate to opposite poles of the cells.
Non-kinetochore microtubules elongate and overlap, slide pas another, pushing the opposite poles of the cell away from one another

#### Telophase
Daughter chromosomes de-condense into chromatin, forming two daughter nuclei in the parent cell

#### Cytokinesis
-> Division of cytoplasm to form 2 daughter cells, with equal distribution of organelles , cytoplasm, and chromosomes into daughter cells.
Animal cell:
Formation of cleavage furrow in the surface, formed by the contracting ring of microfilaments, punching the parent cell into two daughter cells.
Plant cell:
<mark class="hltr-green">Cell plate formation : golgi vesicles fuse to form cell plates, forming new cell wals and new cell surface memberanes. Cell wall enlarges and extends to completely seperate 2 daughter cells</mark>

> [!Note] Benefit of using same daughter cells in asexual reproduction : Genetic stability

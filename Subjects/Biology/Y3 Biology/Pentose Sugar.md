In this note, we shall observe Pentose sugars in Nucleic acids
There are two types of sugars used in Nucliec acids, namely **Ribose** and **Deoxyribose**.
![[Pasted image 20260812112525.png]]

### Ribose
There is hydroxyl group on the second carbon, which increases the reactivity  of the sugar, hence, necluic acids made with ribose are less stable and mutate more

### Deoxyribose
There is only a H atom on the second carbon , hence the sugar is more stable, hence nucleic acuds made with deoxyribose are more stable.
DNA is made with de oxy ribose



The linking of pentose sugars occurs through a [[Condensation reaction]]. to form a phosphodiester bond.

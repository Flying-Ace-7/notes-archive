[[Biology MOC]]

Insulin is a hormone secreted by the $\beta$- cells of the islets of Langerhans in the pancreas. 

Insulin is a vital hormone, whose function includes:
 - When there is a high glucose concentration in the blood, to maintain homeostasis
	- stimulating the <mark class="hltr-red">conversion of glucose into glucose</mark> for storage
	- Increasing permeability of cell membranes to glucose and<mark class="hltr-red"> increase the rate of glucose </mark>uptake by cells
	- Increasing <mark class="hltr-red">amount of oxidation</mark> of glucose during tissue respiration

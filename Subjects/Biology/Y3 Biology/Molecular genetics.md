### DNA Replication

1. DNA unwinds
2. Enzymes (DNA Helicase) unzips DNA
	1. Hydrogen bonding between nitrogenous bases broken
3. Both Strands are used as template and read by **DNA polymerase** (Replicates from 3' end to 5' end)
4. DNA is made using free floating nucleaotides, via complementary base pairing
5. NEw strand formend and rezipped by DNA

### Transcription
 1. <mark class="hltr-yellow">RNA Polymerase</mark> unwinds DNA
 2. Uses on strand as the template strand
 3. Using complementary base pairing and free-floating nucleotides, a strand of mRNA is produced , which travels (Migrate) out of the nuclear envelope

> [!Note] Different forms of RNA
> - mRNA: Carries genetic information for translation
> - rRNA: make up structure of ribosome
> - tRNA: Transfer specific amino acids to ribosome during translation, (using codons)

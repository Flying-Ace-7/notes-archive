### Regulation of blood glucose level

How is blood glucose level increased?
Well, there are a few ways:
The most obvious one being to secrete glucagon, to stiumlate the converison of glucagon to glucose.
There are other ways too:
- Ketogenesis of fat (breaking up of fatty acid bonds), and converting them to energy and releasing alternative fuel into the bloodstream
- Deanimation of amino-acids to form urea and deanimated amino acids, the deanimated acids can be furhter converted to glucose
\

How is blood glucose level decreased?
Well, it is mainly by increasing the secretino of insulin
Insulin helps stimulate the conversion of glucose to glycogen, and increase the permeability of cells to glucose to increase glucose uptake, and increase rate of cellular respiration.

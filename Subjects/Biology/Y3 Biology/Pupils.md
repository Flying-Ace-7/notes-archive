The pupil is a gap in between the [[Iris]] of an [[Eye]] , which allows light to pass through to be refracted by the lens onto the fovea on the retina, allowing for vision.

Some Key points involving the Pupils include the:

### Pupil Cranial reflex
The pupil Cranial reflex is a <mark class="hltr-orange">polysnaptic</mark> reflex. that occurs when there is a sudden change in light level in the surroundings. 
A typical pathway of the reflex is as follows:

1. Stimulus recieved -> Increase in light intensity 
2. Detected by the [[Retina]] (receptor)
3. It sends electrical signals to the Sensroy neurone in the optic nerve
4. The electical signal is conducted to the brain (integration center)
5. An electrical signal is conducted back to the motor neuron
6. And the electrical signal is conducted to the iris muscle, which causes constriction of pupil\


### Dim light condition


When the stimulus of a dim light is detected, Dilation of pupil occurs
The circular muscles surrounding the pupil relax, whiile the radial muscles contract

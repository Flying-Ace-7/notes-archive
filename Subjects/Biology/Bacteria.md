The cell wall is made of peptiidoglycan, helps maintian shape of bacterium, and prevents burtsting due to osmostic pressure.

Plasmid dna -> carry additional genes tht provide bacteria with surviaival advantages.
Capsule -> Outer slippery protective layer surrounding <mark class="hltr-blue">some</mark> bacteria
- helps the bacteria stick to attach and adhere to surfaces/host cells
- protects from immune system,
	- mask the surface antigens located beneath the capsule. 
	- Difficult for immune cells to target these sites, 
	- <mark class="hltr-cyan">provides immune evasion through protected shielding of recognition sites</mark>

Many bacterium also has a flagellum, which os a singl long whiplike structure, made of a **protein** called flagellin, helps with sperm motility.

> [!tips] Benefits of bacterium movement
> - move towards nutrients
> - escape from harmful substances
> - increase survivablity

<mark class="hltr-green">Note: Bacteria have no mitochondria, but they do require ATP, and produce it by aerobic respiration, anaerobic respiration, and fermentation in their plasma membrane</mark>

Plius -> short hair like protein structures, extends from structure of bacterium.
- helps to stick to surfaces or host cells.
- Transfer genetic material between bacteria, gain genetic variation. **Bacterial conjugation**
- (transfer of plasmid dna)

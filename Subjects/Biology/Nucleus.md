[[Biology MOC]]

Nucleus is a structure that is found in all eukrayotic cells except for Mature **Phloem sieve tube elements and Red blooc cells** (erythrocytes)

Usually one nucleus per cell (uninecleated), some cells can be binucleated like _paramecium_ or multinucleated (skeletal muscle fibres)

> [!note] Function of Nucleus
> Direct protein synthesis (by synthesizing mRNA)
> Contain Chromatin
> **Essential for cell division**
> Conatins the hereditary material of an organism


-> <mark class="hltr-cyan">Nuclear Envelope</mark>
Composed of 2 membranes , sepearated by a **fluid filled space**
_The outer layer is continuous with the smooth endoplasmic reticulum_
The nuclear envelope is perforated by nuclear pores, to allow for exchange of substances and migration of RNA between nucleus and cytoplasm

-> <mark class="hltr-blue">Nucleolus</mark>
Site of synthesis of RNA,
most visible structure within a nucleus that is not undergoing division.
Composed of a network of densely stained granules and fibres
**One or more** nucleoli may be found in a nucleus


-> <mark class="hltr-purple">Nucleoplasm</mark>
A nucleoplasm is a **semi-fluid** matrix that fills the nucleus, _serves as a medium of diffusion_ of metabolites and macromolecules

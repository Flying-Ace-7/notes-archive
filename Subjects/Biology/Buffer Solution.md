what is a buffer solution?
A buffer solution helps regulate the pH of a solvent/substance, such that even is certain acidic/basic compounds are added, the pH is not affected significantly

> [!expample] $H_2CO_3$ is a buffers solution in our blood
> $H_2CO_3$ dissociates accordingly
> $$
> H_{2}CO_{3} \rightleftharpoons HCO_{3}^- + H^+
> $$
> where the equations shifts left when blood is alkaline, and shifts right when blood is acidic.
> $HCO_{3}^-$ acts as a proton acceptor

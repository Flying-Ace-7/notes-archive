(3) Activated G protein diffueses along the membrane binds and activates an enzyme .

Note: Any mutation in any of these proteins will render this signal transmission ineffective.


### RTK Signalling
![[Pasted image 20260929165445.png]]Function as tyrosine kinase which transfers a phosphate group from ATP to tyrosine on a substrate protein.

Activated (Phosphorylated) receptor tyrosine-kinase adds a __phosphate group__ from ATP to tyrosine on a substrate protein. Activated relay proteins trigger many different transduction pathways and responses.


> [!note] GPCR vs RTK
> [[Subjects/Biology/Y3 Biology/Glucagon|Glucagon]] binds to GPCR
> [[Subjects/Biology/Y3 Biology/Insulin|Insulin]] binds to RTK.
> Kinases like RTK add phosphates onto other proteins.

**In an insulin receptor (RTK), it will trigger glucose transporters to be inserted into the plasma membrane. Insulin is also needed to trigger cell growth and differentiation**

## Food intake control

![[Pasted image 20260929172120.png]]
Step 1:
- Stretch signals aginst the stomach walls, triggers release of Gastrin
- Gastrin circulats via bloodstream back to stomach, to stimualte production of gastric juices
Step 2:
- Chyme enters duodenum 
- The duodenum releases digestive hormones cholecystokinin (CCK) and secretin.
- CCK -> Stimualtes release of digestive enzymes from pancreas and bile from gallbladder
- Secretun -> Stimulates pancreas to release bicarbonate ($HCO_3^-$) neutralising chyme

Step 3: (If chyme is rich in FATS) ()
- High levels of secretin + CCK released acts on stomach -> **inhibits peristalisis and secretion fo gastric juices**-> Slowing digestion , give more time for bile to act

> [!tip] Insulin acts on all cells except brain cells
> Brain cells need a lot of glucose, to not inhibit the amount of glucose those cells obtain

### Regulation of appetite and consumption
Ghrelin: Hunger Hormone

secreted by the stomach wall, ghrelin is one of the signals that triggers feelings of hunger as mealtimes approach. IN dieters who lose weigt, ghrelin levels increase, which may be the reason ir is hard to stay on a diet.

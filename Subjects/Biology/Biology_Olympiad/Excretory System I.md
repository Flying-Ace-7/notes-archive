### Structure of the kidney tubule

There are <mark class="hltr-yellow">two types of kidney tubules</mark>  ,<mark class="hltr-cyan"> Cortical nephron </mark>and <mark class="hltr-green">juxtamedullary nephron</mark>

#### Cortical nephron
Location: Located primarily in the outer cortex
	its short loop barely dips into the outer medulla
Blood Supply from Peritubular capillaries .
Function : Primarily incolved in filtration and the reabsorbtion of solutes, plays a role in standard urine production.
Comprises approximately 85% of all nephrons in the human kidney

#### Juxtamedullary nephron
Location: 
	Located near the border of te cortex and medulla
	Long loop of henle extends deep into the medulla
Blood supply rom _vasa recta_ and peritubular capillaries.
Function
- Crucial for concentrating urine and _conserving water_. 
- Helps creates the osmotic gradient in the medulla, **necessary for reabsorbing water from the collecting ducts**
Comprises aproximately 15% of all nephrons, plays a critical role in <mark class="hltr-blue">maintaining water balance and producing concentrated urine</mark> 


### What are the countercurrent mechanisms in kidneys

	- countercurrent multiplier
		- Opposite Flow of **filtrate** in the descending limb and ascending loop of henle
	- Countercurrent exchanger
		- Opposite flow of **blood** in descending portion and ascending portion of vasa recta

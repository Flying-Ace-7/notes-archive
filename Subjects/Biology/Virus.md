[[Biology MOC]]

Itracellular parasites,
<mark class="hltr-red">Unable to reproduce independently.
Can only replicate inside living host cells.</mark>

Contains nucleic acid enclosed within a protein coat.
Two main types of viruses: **Non-enveloped** vs **Enveloped** viruses.

> Nucleic acid
> Either your [[RNA]] or [[DNA]]
> Why RNA?
> - Mutates more often less stable
> - Allows viruses to adapt and evolve more quickly
> Why DNA?
> - DNA is more stable and mutate less often

They can be either non-enveloped virus, or enveloped virus.

### Structure of non-enveloped virus

-> Example HPV


They have a nuclocaspid surrounding the capsid (protein coat), whcich contains the nucliec acid. They lack a lipid membrane surrounding the capsid.
The spike proteins are found on the capsipd

> [!tip] What type of nucleic acid do the viruses contain. 
> RNA or DNA : Either single standed/double stranded DNA or single stranded/double stranded RNA

### Structure of enveloped virus

Cotain a lipid envelope around the nucleocaspid, containing the spike proteins.

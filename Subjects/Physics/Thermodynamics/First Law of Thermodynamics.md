The first law of thermodynamics is a fundamental principle stating that ==**energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed from one form to another**==. It establishes that the total internal energy of an isolated system is constant, and any change in energy is accounted for by heat transferred and work done.

The university constituites as a closed isolated system, where the total energy of the universe remains constant.

> Chemical energy **lost** by the system= Energy **gained** by surroundings
>$$
\Delta E_{\text{sysetm}} =- \Delta E_{\text{surroundings}}
$$


It can be expressed as 

$$
\Delta U = Q+W
$$
where:
- $\Delta U$ is the change in the system's internal energy
- $Q$ is the heat added to the system
- $W$ is the work done **on** the system / by the surroundings

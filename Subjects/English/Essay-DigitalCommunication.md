COMMENTS:

The signposting is generally clear, and the conclusion returns to the main arguments effectively.

The rehook with Mr Azman is a good structural technique because it gives the essay a sense of completion.

Some paragraphs explain how social media facilitates communication, rather than how it enables young people to communicate more positively. Establish what positive communication means. For example: supportive communication, greater empathy, greater inclusivity etc. Just saying that young people can "communicate more" does not necessarily mean they communicate more positively. This distinction is particularly important in Points 1 and 2.

## Education
- **Handwriting vs. Typing:** A 2025 analysis by [Edutopia](https://www.edutopia.org/visual-essay/the-10-most-significant-education-studies-of-2025/) found that children who practice writing by hand achieve **92% accuracy** in letter naming (compared to **75%** for typing) and write new letters accurately more than twice as often (**64% vs. 28%**). Brain scans show handwriting triggers deeper learning and memory retention across all grade levels. [[1](https://www.edutopia.org/visual-essay/the-10-most-significant-education-studies-of-2025/)]

- **Spaced Practice and Retrieval:** Research on learning science shows that studying material once followed by active retrieval (such as flashcards, quizzes, or summarizing from memory) yields much stronger long-term retention than reading or studying material twice consecutively. Spacing out practice sessions across weeks also boosts follow-up test scores significantly compared to cramming in a single day. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vn4qTwt1x2E&t=4)]

- **Gamification in Classrooms:** A study from Singapore's Ministry of Education ([MOE Completed Studies](https://www.moe.gov.sg/education-in-sg/educational-technology-journey/rapid-research/completed-studies)) on Primary 5 students showed that <mark class="hltr-orange">content-based gamification</mark> (where game mechanics like points and badges directly mirror the storyline) strongly supports learning achievements and intrinsic motivation better than <mark class="hltr-cyan">generic gamification</mark>. [[1](https://www.moe.gov.sg/education-in-sg/educational-technology-journey/rapid-research/completed-studies)]

- **EdTech and Digital Literacy:** A Stanford-led meta-analysis highlights that digital learning tools are not equally effective; their impact depends heavily on specific design features and the foundational literacy or numeracy skills they target. [[1](https://ed.stanford.edu/news/new-study-explores-what-makes-edtech-tools-more-or-less-effective)]

- **Holistic Development vs. Parental Stress:** Research from the Institute of Policy Studies in Singapore indicates that while parents increasingly value character values and social-emotional growth over raw grades, **75%** worry their children will fall behind, and nearly **90%** feel children spend too much time on tuition and enrichment. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3vvDPoXwk3w)]

## Elderly

- **Social Disconnection While Living with Family:** A Singapore study led by Professor Koh Woon Puay found that **6% of seniors** were socially disconnected, and of those, **more than 78% (804 out of 1,021)** actually lived with their families. Many lacked someone they could turn to for help. [[1](https://www.straitstimes.com/singapore/elderly-experience-social-disconnection-even-among-those-living-with-their-family-study)]

- **Impact of Family Tie Quality vs. Proximity:** Research from the National Health and Aging Trends Study (and highlighted by the [Institute for Social Research](https://isr.umich.edu/news-events/news-releases/new-paper-explores-the-impact-of-family-ties-on-older-adults/)) shows that **kinless** or **disconnected** older adults (aged 70+) have lower mental health scores, whereas **strong family tie quality** matters far more for well-being than physical proximity. [[1](https://isr.umich.edu/news-events/news-releases/new-paper-explores-the-impact-of-family-ties-on-older-adults/), [2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10699742/)]

- **The Realities of Informal Caregiving:** Data from the Singapore Survey on Informal Caregiving indicates that **20% of family caregivers** looking after seniors aged 75 and older are themselves **over the age of 65**, with **5% being spousal caregivers aged 80 and older**. [[1](https://www.duke-nus.edu.sg/docs/librariesprovider3/research-policy-brief-docs/a-profile-of-older-caregivers-in-singapore.pdf?sfvrsn=be6b7e59_0)]

- **Young Family Caregivers:** A qualitative study on young adult caregivers in Singapore (aged 23 to 29) highlighted severe **role conflicts** across work, studies, and financial strain, alongside intergenerational friction and psychological burdens. [[1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10888348/)]

- **Intergenerational Support and Health:** A study analyzing the China Health and Retirement Longitudinal Study (CHARLS) revealed that children providing **spiritual comfort** positively boosted both the physical and mental health of seniors, whereas direct **life care support** sometimes correlated with higher psychological stress or negative self-assessments due to a loss of independence. [[1](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2405844024157404)]

- **Spousal Support and Life Satisfaction:** A cross-sectional analysis from the Health and Retirement Study indicates that **spousal or partner support** is the single strongest predictor of life satisfaction among older adults—more influential than support from children, extended relatives, or friends

## MY EXs for Malaya Deco

### WWII
Defeating <mark class="hltr-green">White man superiority complex</mark>, + resistance by Japanese occupation
1. British defeated by japanese swiftly, south east asia occupied by japanese 6 months after pearl harbour -> lack of credible defense for british
2. britain fought in europe and asia during wwII, causing huge financial impacts and debts to the USA.
	- They needed to reduce expenditure and maintain malaya as a profitable colony, to rebuild britain's economy and repay debt
-> Increase confidence in locals to wage war against british, increase desire for independence.



Chinese were treated very harshly and had brutal treatment by the japanese in malaya.
> The chinese in south east asia supported the chinese side in the Second Sino-japanese war financially, and the japanese brutally treated the chinese as 'revenge'

<mark class="hltr-red">Growth of local nationalist groups</mark>
- This harsh treatments created locla nationalist groups to resist the japanese rule. Many of these groups persisted after the japanese occupation, to fight for the independence of Malaya.
- Example: MPAJA, founded on Decempber 1941, by the MCP. They waged guerilla war against the japanese.
- The MPAJA received trainign and military supplies from the allies, many of which was kept after the occupation


### Collaboration with Japanese
The japanese collabortaed with locals, mainly the malays, and giving them high positions in civil sevice previously held by high-ranking europeans. Locals recruited as civil servants
The locals gain administrative experience, increase desire for independace.

The japanese made the europeans POWs, and paraded them in cities, dispelling WMS, and increasing confidence in locals that they could be beaten, increasing nationalism. 
They also grew paramilitary groups like PETA in indonesia 

Increasing Desire for Self Rule – Spread of Western Education,spread Western political ideals which were banned in SEA. Increase resentment against colonial powers

Overall, increase nationalims, increase anti-colonial sentiments

-> MPAJA started targetting ethnic malay villages and killing them, for 'siding' with the japanese after the JO ended.

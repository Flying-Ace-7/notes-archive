### Reason for formation of Alliance

- Ethinc Unity and Cooperation
	- Unite the major ethni groups under one political umberlla, promoting cooperation and unity among them
- Political stability
- Economic Development

Now, the MIC joined the ==Alliance==in 1954. (Malayan Indian congress, 1946)

**1955 Elections Victory (Federal) 51/52 seats won, with 81% of votes won**
Formed the federal government of Malaya under <mark class="hltr-blue">Tunku</mark> . Of alliance's seats, 34 were held by malays, 15 by Chinese, and 2 by indians.

There also was a executive council of (6 Malayan, 3 chinese headed government, and 5 british officials). The leaders of the Alliance pushed for self-government (all policy in the hands of the Malayans, except for foreign affairs and defence).


Alliance party will lead the Baling talks, even though it is a failure, it shows that the Alliance were able to get rid of the Communists and that they were stable without colonial assistance. 
They gained the right for independance from the British,a nd drafted the Merdeka constituition.

>[!danger] What is Nationalism
>- the desire for _Independance_ and Sovereignty
>- Sovereignty - right to govern/make decisions without external influence
>- **Sense of Belonging**
>	- Patriotism
>	- Pride of National _identity_
>This pride creates Unity among people.
> Through Colonialism however, and the general oppression amongst the people, hatred rises for the colonial powers, raising anti-colonial sentiments.
> This _unites_ the people in order to get rid of the colonial powers.
> (These are called Proto-nationalist movements)

## Changing powers of international power structures

#### Pre- WWI
The world is made up of empires. Military prowess determined the might of the empire. They set up colonies for economic wealth and trade.

#### In between WWI and WWII
The war ([[War I]])extended for longer than expected, empires couldn't sustain their military. Empires started to disintegrated.
Formation of new nation states, often among arbitary lines. USA emerged as a _hegemon_, a leadership within a system of competing states.
Germany and Japan realise this a good time to expand to be considered a world power, leading to Rise of Hitler etc.
Rising threats from Germany in europe and Japan in Asia


#### Post WWII

USA and USSR - hegemon
(expansion vs containment)

Europe in ruins after [WWII]([[WWII and Cold War]]) .
UN was set up. Global movement towards self-determination. (_strong independance movement_ )

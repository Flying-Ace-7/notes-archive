Today, we shall be exploring a new type of essay _le weighing essay_ 

> [!Example] Example qn
> "The MCP mainly acted out of <mark class="hltr-red">grievances</mark> rather than <mark class="hltr-red">aspirations</mark> to govern Malaya in their <mark class="hltr-orange">instigation of the insurgency</mark>"

<mark class="hltr-orange"> </mark> - Outcome
<mark class="hltr-red"> </mark> - Given Factor 1/2


We need to match the correct factor to the given factors, and show how they lead to the outcome. (Cause for the insurgency in this case)
// Paragraph 1

Giver Factor 1:  Grievance

P : The Mcp mainly acted out of the grievances they experienced in the Japanese occupation, and British return to instigate the communist insurgency
Ex1: Japanese target Chinese due to their support during the second sino-jap war. <mark class="hltr-blue">Used the Malays to form police to attack Chinese</mark>.
Ex2: Federation of Malaya -> british favoured malays listened to UMNO's demands -> implemented stricter citizenship laws against non-malays despite the hartal/strike carried out by the AMCJA-PUTERA alliance .

EXP: The British were biased against non-malays, and supproted the Malays who "_betrayed_" them during JO . There was hence increased aggression towards the British, by the MCP, causing a communist insurgency.

// Paragraph 2
Given Factor 2: Aspirations
P : The goal of the MCP was to create a communist government  <mark class="hltr-green">by getting rid of a colonial influence.</mark> 

Ex1: Strikes against AMCJA
Ex2: Ignore order to disam, and fought for legitimisation of the party.

EXP: Wanted to achieve their goas, refused to compromise during the baling talks. 

// Paragraph 3 : Weighing of Given factor 1 vs Givern Factor 2
we can say _for example_: Root cause: 
Aspirations was the main cause of the Communist insurgency, as the Aspirations was the root casue for the grievances felt by the MCP (BoC)
> It's basically like doing Balanced Conclusion
Exp: Due to the aspirations for the MCP to <mark class="hltr-cyan">protect the legitimacy of their party</mark> , and ensure <mark class="hltr-cyan">the rights of the Communists</mark> who were mainly CHinese. The Actions caused by the <mark class="hltr-yellow">British Federation of Malaya Proposal</mark> and implementation direclty casused the grief since it contradiced their goals. <mark class="hltr-orange">The stricter citizenship  laws </mark>showed their disregard for the chinese rights and their refusal to acknowledge the MCP as a legitimate party showed that they had to rely on aggresion for their goals to be heard . (by replicating UMNO's aggression but increased.)

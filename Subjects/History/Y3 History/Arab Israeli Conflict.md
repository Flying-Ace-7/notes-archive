### War of 1956
The egyptian government wanted to nationalise the Suez canal, bring it under their own control.

The 1956 Suez War was a joint Israeli-British-French operation, in which Israel invaded the [Sinai Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinai_Peninsula "Sinai Peninsula") and British and French forces landed at the port of [Suez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suez "Suez"), ostensibly to separate the warring parties, though the real motivation of Great Britain and [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France "France") was to protect the interests of investors in those countries who were affected by Egyptian President [Nasser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamel_Abdel_Nasser "Gamel Abdel Nasser")'s decision to [nationalize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationalize "Nationalize") the [Suez Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suez_Canal "Suez Canal").
